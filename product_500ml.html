Our Journey

Farm to Home, One Honest Step at a Time

From the cow to your kitchen — follow every step of how our ghee is made, the traditional Bilona way.

Step 01

Our Gir Cow Farms

Our journey begins at our farms, where indigenous Gir cows are raised with proper nutrition, ethical care, and a natural lifestyle. Their well-being is the foundation of our pure A2 milk.

🐄 Indigenous Gir Cows 🐄 Ethical Animal Care 🌿 Natural Farm Environment
Step 02

Fresh A2 Milk Collection

Fresh A2 milk is collected daily with care to preserve its freshness and natural quality before the Bilona process begins.

🥛 Fresh A2 Milk 🤲 Collected Daily ✨ Naturally Pure
Step 03

Milk is Naturally Set into Curd

Unlike commercial ghee, we first convert fresh milk into curd through natural fermentation. This traditional step forms the foundation of authentic Bilona Ghee.

🥣 Natural Fermentation 🏺 Fresh Curd Preparation 🌿 No Direct Cream Separation
Stp 04

Traditional Bilona Method

The curd is slowly hand-churned using a traditional wooden Bilona to naturally separate fresh makkhan (butter) from the buttermilk.

🪵 Slow Hand Churning 🧈 Fresh Makkhan Separation Wooden Bilona Process
Step 05

Slow-Cooked into Golden Ghee

The freshly churned makkhan is gently heated over a slow flame until it transforms into rich, golden Bilona Ghee with its authentic aroma and flavour.

🔥 Slow Flame Cooking 🧈 Rich Aroma & Flavour ✨ Pure Golden Ghee
Step 06

Quality Checks

Before it reaches your home, every batch undergoes rigorous quality checks to ensure it meets our standards of purity, consistency, and freshness.

🫙 90+ Quality Checks 🤲 Purity Verified 💛 Batch Teste
Step 07

Carefully Packed

Once approved, the pure Bilona Ghee is hygienically packed in premium food-grade jars to preserve its freshness, rich aroma, and authentic taste until it reaches your home.

❤️ Hygienically Packed 🐄 Freshness Sealed 🏆 Premium Food-Grade Jars
Step 08

From our farms to your family's kitchen!

Every jar is carefully delivered, carrying the purity of tradition and the goodness of authentic Bilona Ghee.

Farm to Family Pan-India Delivery Delivered with Care

Not high yield. Not lower cost. Just flavour, nutrition, and soul.

Our Story

You Ask, We Answer

A. Our A2 Desi Cow Ghee is made entirely from the milk of indigenous Gir cows. The cows are neither forced nor injected with hormones. Our ghee is bi-directionally bilona churned with a wooden churner, preserving all natural nutrients. Hence A2 Desi Cow Ghee is costlier and better than regular ghee.
A. Yes. We source exclusively from verified A2 Gir cows in Gujarat.
A. Bilona is an ancient Vedic method where curd is churned bi-directionally using a wooden churner, then slow-cooked into ghee. This preserves all natural nutrients and gives the ghee its distinctive granular texture.
