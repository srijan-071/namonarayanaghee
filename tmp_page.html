The Vedic Method

The Ancient Art of Bilona

100% Pure Ingredients

Because you deserve the best

01

Boiling

Fresh A2 milk is gently boiled over a slow wood fire, preserving its natural essence.

02

Curdling

The milk is naturally curdled overnight using traditional methods.

03

Churning (Bilona)

The curd is hand-churned in a wooden bilona to separate pure makhana (butter).

04

Heating

The makhana is slowly heated on a low flame until the water evaporates, leaving behind pure, aromatic ghee.
